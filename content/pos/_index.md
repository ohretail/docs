Oh! Retail POS runs your registers, your catalog, and the back office from one
store database. These guides take you from your first sign-in to a register
that keeps selling when the internet goes down.

**New to Oh! Retail?** Start with [Set up your store](store-setup.md). It walks
through each first-day task in order and links to the detailed guides.

## Two ways to run a register

Browser register
: Open your store's Oh! Retail site in a browser on the register PC. Nothing
  to install, but the register needs an internet connection to take sales.

Installed register
: A Windows app that keeps its own copy of the store database, so it keeps
  taking sales through an internet outage and catches up when the connection
  returns. See [Installed registers](local-register/_index.md).

You can mix both in one store, register by register.

## What the status labels mean

Each guide shows how finished the feature it describes is, so you know before
you plan around it.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Available | In daily use by stores. |
| Pilot | Running in real stores, with our team checking in. Expect changes, and tell us what you find. |
| Sandbox only | Built and tested against a provider's test system. Not used for real payments yet. |
